>
> **What it does:** crypto prices, token security checks, contract risk analysis, and full on-chain investigations.
>
> **How payment works:** standard x402 — the client (human or AI agent) gets a 402 with payment requirements, pays USDC on Base to the specified address, and retries with the `X-PAYMENT` header. No signup, no API keys, no accounts.
>
> **What I care about:** the verification is real. I do on-chain verification of the payment (recipient, amount, anti-replay) rather than trusting the header — the early version was vulnerable to spoofed payments and I rewrote it to fail-closed.
